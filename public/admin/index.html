🎓 End of School Year

Use this button at the end of the school year to clear all children and registrations. Parent and counselor accounts will be kept — only children, registrations, absences, and attendance records will be deleted.

After clearing, simply upload next year's roster to start fresh. Parents will see their new children and updated information.

⚠️ This action cannot be undone. All attendance history, absences, and child registrations will be permanently deleted.
⚠️ Wipe All Data (Testing Only)

Use this to reset the app for testing. Deletes ALL parents, counselors, children, activities, calendar events, and invitations. Admin accounts and schools are preserved.

Flow: wipe → upload a fake roster → test (including "Send All Invitations" — emails only go to the fake addresses) → wipe again → upload the real roster.

🚨 IRREVERSIBLE. Everything except admins and schools will be permanently deleted.
