Built for everyone in the program

One sign-in. The app knows who you are and shows you only what you need.

For parents

Everything about your child’s afternoon, in a few taps.

  • Tap “I’m here” when you arrive — no calling, no waiting
  • Report an absence from the calendar in seconds
  • See who’s attending today, and read program announcements

For counselors

The day’s roster and the curb, on one screen.

  • Take attendance with one tap per child
  • See waiting parents live, and claim a pickup so nobody doubles up
  • Request a day off and track its approval

For administrators

The whole program, without a spreadsheet.

  • Attendance and absences by day, searchable and exportable
  • Manage families, counselors and schools in one place
  • Review day-off requests and send announcements to every parent

Designed for the moment it matters

Pickup happens in a fifteen-minute window with a line of cars. Everything else is built around getting that right.

Instant, not eventually

A parent taps and it lands on the counselor’s screen immediately — with how long they have been waiting, so the longest wait goes first.

Every program stays separate

Each organization’s families are isolated at the database level, not by a filter someone might forget to write.

Works on the phone in your hand

Install it to the home screen and it behaves like an app. Parents and counselors are standing outside, not sitting at a desk.

Ready when your program is

Sign in with the email your program has on file.
